Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026

Novixo respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly and transparently.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Novixo ("Novixo", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when users interact with Novixo services, websites, applications, or integrations with third-party platforms, including social media platforms such as TikTok.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on the services you use and the permissions you authorize, we may process information such as:

  • Basic account or profile information provided by a connected platform.
  • Platform-specific user identifiers.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens required to maintain authorized connections.
  • Content and media that a user chooses to prepare or publish.
  • Publication metadata, status information and technical identifiers.
  • Technical information necessary to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

3. TikTok integration

When a user connects a TikTok account to an authorized Novixo application, TikTok may provide information and permissions approved by that user through TikTok's OAuth authorization process.

Depending on the permissions granted, Novixo may use this authorization to identify the connected TikTok account and to prepare or submit content for publication through TikTok's official APIs.

Novixo does not receive or store a user's TikTok password. Authentication is handled by TikTok through its official authorization flow.

4. How we use information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide requested application functionality.
  • Connect authorized social media accounts.
  • Prepare, submit and track user-authorized content publications.
  • Maintain authentication with supported platforms.
  • Protect the security and integrity of the service.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve reliability.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

5. Content publication

Novixo integrations may provide functionality that allows authorized users to prepare and submit media for publication to connected social media accounts.

Content is submitted only through authorized platform interfaces and according to the permissions granted by the account owner.

6. OAuth tokens and credentials

Access tokens, refresh tokens and similar authorization credentials may be stored when necessary to maintain a user's authorized connection to a third-party service.

We take reasonable technical measures to protect these credentials and restrict their use to authorized application functionality.

7. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party platforms when necessary to perform functionality explicitly requested by the user, such as publishing content to an authorized social media account.

We may also use service providers where necessary for hosting, security, storage or other infrastructure functions.

8. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, or fulfill legitimate operational requirements.

Authorization credentials may be deleted or invalidated when an account is disconnected or when access is revoked.

9. Revoking platform access

Users may revoke access granted to Novixo through the security or connected-app settings provided by the relevant third-party platform.

Revoking authorization may prevent connected functionality from continuing to operate.

10. Data deletion requests

Users may request deletion of personal information associated with Novixo by contacting:

novixohq@gmail.com

Please include enough information to identify the relevant account or integration. We may need to verify the request before completing it.

11. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

12. Third-party services

Novixo may integrate with third-party services including TikTok, Meta, Facebook and Instagram. Those platforms operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

This Privacy Policy does not control how third-party platforms independently collect or process information.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, integrations, operational practices or legal obligations change.

The current version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
